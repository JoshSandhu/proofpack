## What landed

**1. A composite case key walked straight through the X2 guard and rendered DeLong.**
The guard tested `ids.shape[0] != n_rows` while its own docstring stated the invariant
as "`cluster_ids[i]` is the case of row `i`" — which needs a *one-dimensional column of
exactly `n_rows` ids*. `shape[0]` is one dimension short of that, so
`df[['patient','visit']].to_numpy()` — shape `(400, 2)` — satisfied the guard and
carried no row-to-case correspondence at all. Measured at `ace5cf5` on 200 patients ×
2 lesions, ρ = 0.9: `delong_wald (0.6488, 0.7510)` and `wilson (0.6020, 0.6951)` with
`analytic_status: used`, `route: none`, `flags: []`, against the cluster bootstrap's
`(0.6279, 0.7696)` and `(0.5850, 0.7125)` on the same rows — **1.387 and 1.370 times
too narrow**, no W13, no companion refusal. That is objection X2's symptom exactly,
reached by the next argument shape.

`_require_aligned_ids` is replaced by `_case_column`, now the only `asarray` in the
module: it refuses any array that is not one id per row (rank) and, where the row count
is known, any array that is not exactly `n_rows` of them (length). It is called from
`plan_clustering` in **both** argument forms, from `_resolved`, and from
`clustered_by_case` and `clustered_flat`, which are public and were reachable directly.

*Pinned by* `test_a_cluster_id_array_that_is_not_one_id_per_row_is_refused` (8 params),
`test_the_two_argument_plan_also_refuses_a_key_that_is_not_a_case_column` (4),
`test_a_composite_case_key_renders_no_delong_and_no_wilson` (4),
`test_the_public_resamplers_refuse_a_key_that_is_not_a_case_column` (4). Pre-fix,

## What did not land, and why

**X-4 — the version-comparison and operating-point paths have no clustering guard, and
cannot get one today.** `stats.discrimination.paired_delong` — the statistic the PCCP
performance-evaluation report rests on — and `stats.proportions.two_by_two_metrics`
take **no `cluster_ids` argument at all**, reach no guard, and will emit DeLong, Wilson
and Newcombe intervals over clustered rows with no flag and no refusal. Verified this
session: neither is called anywhere in `src` yet. Making `paired_delong`
clustering-aware needs a **cluster bootstrap of the difference** `AUC(A) − AUC(B)`,
which is a build-day feature and not a docstring — inventing it inside a repair round
would be exactly the "half of it is worse than none of it" this module already refuses
over BCa. What landed instead: the sentence in `discrimination.py` that read as
covering that file's outputs is corrected, both functions say the gap in their own
docstrings, and a test asserts they say it *and* that neither has quietly gained a
clustering parameter without one.

**Question for you, answerable in one line:** should day 5 (a) refuse to emit a version
comparison at all when the table is clustered, or (b) emit it with a typed
`clustered_data_analytic_ci_invalid` refusal in place of the interval, until the
clustered paired statistic exists? I would take (b) — it matches what `auroc_ci`
already does — but it is a reporting decision, so it is yours.

**Still open by construction, unchanged and unhidden:** when `cluster_ids` is not passed
at all, `stats.bootstrap` has no evidence of clustering in its inputs and renders the
analytic interval. Nothing in that module can detect a case column it was never given.
That guarantee has to come from the day-5 caller that reads the customer's table, on
every cell. It is in the module docstring under "What the guard does not check, and
cannot" and pinned by
`test_cluster_ids_never_passed_at_all_are_still_invisible_to_this_module`, which asserts
the *current* behaviour so the paragraph cannot quietly stop being true.

## Tomorrow needs

Day 5 — the caller that reads the customer's table and drives these cells — inherits
three things:

1. **It must pass `cluster_ids` on every cell.** The module cannot detect a case column
   it was never given, and it renders the analytic interval when it is absent. This is
   the whole remaining half of X2 and it now lives entirely in day 5.
2. **It must pass a one-dimensional case column of exactly the cell's rows.** A
   composite key must be reduced to a single id per row *by the caller* — the engine
   refuses it rather than flattening it, because flattening invents a correspondence
   the customer did not supply. Subgroup cells need the ids **sliced with the rows**;
   handing a 400-row run-level array to a 120-row cell now raises.
3. **It must carry clustering for three surfaces, not one.** `auroc_ci` and
   `proportion_ci` are routed. `paired_delong` (version comparison / PCCP) and
   `two_by_two_metrics` (every operating-point proportion) are not, and cannot be until
   the decision in "What did not land" is made.

Nothing else in the engine changed. `Number` is untouched, the flag and method enums are
untouched, the F3 and F9 fixtures are untouched, and `git diff --name-only ace5cf5 HEAD
-- tests/` lists exactly one file, `tests/test_bootstrap.py`, so the day-1 to day-3 test
files are unchanged.

   question, not an engine one.

   repair* rather than the code the repair fixed. Is it worth a standing rule that every
   guard docstring names a counter-example that was actually run? Two tests already do
   this by hand.

   found the one part of this repair that was decorative, and it took under four minutes
