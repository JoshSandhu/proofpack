**It has been the same interval every round, and it is now closed properly.** The defect
is a confidence interval that is far too narrow on the multi-lesion design — one patient,
several lesions — in a pack a customer hands to a regulator.

* The **build** refused that cell for the wrong reason, and in doing so let one
  mixed-outcome patient delete a 200-patient interval (round 1, D2).
* The **round-1 repair** fixed that by counting a mixed case towards both outcome classes,
  and the count let through a class supplied by one pure case and one mixed case: two
  units on paper, **zero** resampling variation, coverage 0.233 at a nominal 0.95
  (round 2, R2-D1).
* The **round-2 repair** replaced the count with a test on the largest stratum supplying
  the class — which asks whether the resampler can vary the class **at all**. Round 3
  found the obvious next shape: a class it can *barely* vary. One patient with five
  malignant lesions beside two mixed patients passes that floor with 71 % of the positive
  rows identical in every resample, and the engine rendered `0.735 (0.622, 0.847)` where an
  independently written pooled cluster bootstrap gives `(0.397, 0.860)` — **2.1x too
  narrow, coverage 0.34-0.62**. The original build refused every one of those cells.

**Round 3 replaces the qualitative floor with a quantitative one, and this time the
quantity is exact rather than a shape.** A stratum holding one case is drawn one-from-one,
so its rows are frozen into every resample. The variance of a row-weighted statistic over
independent units of weight `w_j` goes as `sum w_j**2`, so
`sum_frozen w**2 / sum_all w**2` is exactly the share of that class's resampling variance
the interval cannot see, and the interval's standard deviation is understated by at most
`1 - sqrt(1 - share)`. The engine refuses above **one fifth** — a tenth of the class's
standard error, which is the most a nominal 95 % interval may knowingly be short by and
still be the interval the pack claims. It is an *upper* bound on the cost, which is why it
is safe to refuse on, and a test measures the bound against a pooled comparator on the same
cohort rather than taking it on trust.

I did not implement the fix the verifier prescribed. It prescribed refusing on the frozen
share of a class's **rows** at a third; I measured that rule and it does not bound the
harm — at 20 lesions in one frozen patient among 60 mixed patients the row share is 0.25,
under the proposed threshold, and coverage is **0.605 with no precision tier at all**. §2
carries the measurements. The variance-weight share catches those shapes because it is the
quantity the mechanism actually operates on.

**Cost of the stricter rule, measured, not estimated.** Over a 4,196-cell sweep at
`e87843d` and at this commit: **0 point estimates moved, 0 surviving intervals moved, 0
precision tiers lost, 0 refusals turned back into intervals.** 218 AUROC cells go from an
interval to `insufficient_clusters`, and for every one of the 218 I measured, over 300
draws, that the named class really does freeze rows — **zero over-refusals**. No proportion
cell changed at all.

Nothing was refused as unfixable in any round. Repair commit: see `git log -1`.

### R3-D1 — a class dominated by a frozen stratum still rendered an interval 2.1x too narrow
*(fresh attack FA2-B1 — the same defect, mechanism and severity as R2-D1, which round 2 closed only at its endpoint)*

`Resampler.deficient_class` refused a class only when the **largest** stratum supplying it
held fewer than two units. That is the condition under which the class cannot vary at all.
A stratum of one unit is drawn one-from-one whatever the *other* strata do, so a class can
clear the floor and still be mostly frozen:

```
one patient with 5 malignant lesions, two patients with one malignant + one benign,
forty patients with one benign lesion.  43 patients, 49 lesions, --offline, no misuse:

  units_per_stratum : {'positive': 1, 'negative': 40, 'mixed': 2}
  class_strata      : {'positive': (1, 2), 'negative': (40, 2)}
  deficient_class   : None                      <- e87843d: renders
  rendered          : 0.7347  (0.6224, 0.8469)  width 0.2245
  flags             : ['delong_refused_clustered', 'very_low_precision', 'imprecise']
  positive rows present in EVERY resample : 5 of 7 (71 %)
  oracle pooled cluster bootstrap         : (0.3971, 0.8602) width 0.4631  -> 2.1x too narrow
  same cohort at 5bc69fb (the original build) : REFUSED, insufficient_clusters
```

Coverage over 250 replications at a true lesion-level AUROC of `Phi(1/sqrt2) = 0.760250`,
rho = 0.6, MC se ~0.03 — the verifier's numbers on the left, **reproduced independently by
me** on the right before I touched anything:

| shape (pure-pos lesions L, mixed m, 40 neg) | verifier | mine | oracle | tier carried |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L=1 m=2 | 0.618 | 0.649 | 0.804 | very_low_precision |
| L=5 m=2 | 0.440 | 0.460 | 0.820 | very_low_precision |
| L=20 m=10 | 0.412 | 0.424 | 0.920 | **none at all** |

The last row is the worst: 11 positive class units over 51 cases clears both precision
tiers, so the cell carries no annotation whatever while covering the truth 41 % of the time
at a nominal 95 %. And it is the freeze, not small n — matched control, same 20 positive
lesions across the same design, spread over more than one patient: coverage 0.390 -> 0.813,
width 0.1635 -> 0.4051, landing on the oracle.

**Why I did not implement the prescribed fix.** The verifier prescribed refusing when the
frozen share of a class's **rows** reaches a third. I measured that rule before adopting it
and it does not bound the harm, because the row share is blind to how big the frozen
patient is:

```
frozen-share rule on rows, threshold 1/3 — shapes that would still RENDER:
  L=20 m=41  row share 0.328  coverage 0.545  oracle 0.880   no tier
  L=20 m=45  row share 0.308  coverage 0.580  oracle 0.935   no tier
  L=20 m=60  row share 0.250  coverage 0.605  oracle 0.940   no tier
```

`L=20, m=60` is worse than several cells the finding itself calls blockers, and the
row-share rule admits it. The reason is visible in the mechanism: freezing one patient
removes one whole cluster's variance contribution, and variance weight goes as the
**square** of a unit's rows. Measured width understatement against `1 - f_row`:

```
                       f_row   sd ratio vs pooled   1 - f_row (the prescribed model)
  L=1  frozen row       0.050        0.953              0.950   model holds
  L=5  frozen rows      0.050        0.947              0.950   model holds
  L=20 frozen rows      0.050        0.836              0.950   model FAILS by 12 pts
  L=20 frozen rows      0.100        0.798              0.900   model FAILS by 10 pts
```

**Fix.** Keep `max(class_strata[label]) >= MIN_UNITS_PER_STRATUM` — it is exact where the
class has no rows at all, and it is the half a reader checks by counting — and add the
quantitative half on the quantity the mechanism operates on. New
`Resampler.frozen_variance_share`: per class, `sum_frozen w**2 / sum_all w**2` over the
units supplying it, where a unit's weight is the rows of that class it carries (a mixed
case contributes to both). New constant `MAX_FROZEN_VARIANCE_SHARE = 0.20`, refusing with
`insufficient_clusters`, recorded in `BootstrapPolicy.as_dict()["thresholds"]`. The new
rule subsumes the old one — a class every stratum of which is a singleton has a frozen
share of exactly 1 — so both existing multi-lesion fixtures are untouched: 199 pure cases
beside one mixed freeze 1/397 of the positive variance weight, and forty all-mixed cases
freeze none.

The threshold is derived, not tuned: at one fifth of the variance the standard error is
understated by at most `1 - sqrt(0.8) = 0.106`. Every shape I measured came in **under**
that bound, which is the point — it is an upper bound, because for an AUROC the other class
still varies.

**Regression tests.**
- `test_a_class_dominated_by_a_frozen_stratum_is_refused`, eight shapes including the
  untiered `L=20 m=10` and a cohort sitting **exactly** on the threshold. It *measures* the
  freeze rather than asserting it — the positive rows surviving the intersection of 200
  independent draws must be exactly the singleton stratum's — and checks the engine's
  frozen share against the closed form for the cohort. Against `e87843d`:
  `AssertionError: assert None == 'positive'`.
- `test_the_same_rows_spread_over_units_the_resampler_can_vary_still_render`, the matched
  control: the same twenty lesions over two and over five patients still render. If this
  ever fails beside the test above, the floor has gone back to measuring cohort size.
- `test_the_frozen_variance_share_bounds_what_the_freeze_costs`, which measures the bound
  against a pooled cluster bootstrap **on the same cohort** — same rows, same patients,
  same correlation, only the scheme differs — and asserts both that the bound is never
  violated and that the freeze costs something material, so it cannot pass on an unfrozen
  cohort.

### R2-D1 — a class with zero resampling variability rendered an interval

Round 1 replaced a `min`-over-strata floor with a count over the class, so that a mixed

```
distinct positive-row multisets over 500 resamples : 1
```

cells (nine positives among 409 rows carried none: FA-N6).

### D1 — `cluster_ids` supplied with `plan` omitted handed clustered rows to DeLong and Wilson

Against `5bc69fb`: `AssertionError: assert 'used' != 'used'`.

precision tier is taken from the cell's case count, not from the smallest stratum.
