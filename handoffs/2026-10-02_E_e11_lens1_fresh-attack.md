## Blocker

### B1. `wilson_deff` prints with no tier annotation on clustered cells whose measured coverage is 0.70 to 0.89; on strongly unequal case sizes it covers less than the `ad66073` bootstrap did

`proportion_ci` routes every clustered proportion of at least `MIN_CASES_DEFF_WILSON = 5` cases with an estimable design effect to `wilson_deff` (`stats/bootstrap.py` lines 1323-1332). Its flags are `["wilson_refused_clustered", *precision_flags(de.n_cases)]`, and `precision_flags` returns `[]` at 30 cases or more, so such a cell prints with no tier annotation. The bar was measured on one grid only (`design/coverage_deff_wilson.json`): equal case sizes, TAU2 = 0.5, truth 0.5 and 0.9. That run reproduces: my re-run in `wt2` wrote identical rows and threshold 5. My own simulation, written without repo code (ratio-estimator DEFF floored at 1, boundary `sum m_i^2 / n`, Wilson on `n / DEFF`), agrees on the grid cells I re-drew: 0.906 / 0.912 / 0.875 / 0.932 / 0.859 against the committed 0.914 / 0.906 / 0.878 / 0.924 / 0.867. Off the grid, the engine's own `proportion_ci` gives the figures below (`probe/cov_engine*.py`: latent `b_i + e_ij` with Var(b) = TAU2, case sizes fixed, truth p, `plan_clustering("case_id", ...)`). The `ad66073` column is the same driver against `wt0` (cluster bootstrap):

| shape (cases x rows) | p | TAU2 | `2adfaaa` coverage (R) | flags on every cell | `ad66073` coverage (R = 1000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 case of 60 rows + 39 of 1 (K 40, n 99) | 0.90 | 0.5 | **0.6995** (4000) | `wilson_refused_clustered` (+ `imprecise` on 611) | 0.928 |
| 1 case of 40 rows + 29 of 2 (K 30, n 98) | 0.90 | 0.5 | **0.8548** (4000) | the same (+ `imprecise` on 751) | 0.923 |
| 30 x 20 (n 600) | 0.98 | 0.5 | **0.8890** (4000) | `wilson_refused_clustered` only | 0.834 |
| 30 x 20 (n 600) | 0.98 | 0.8 | **0.8020** (4000) | `wilson_refused_clustered` only (1 `imprecise`) | 0.707 |

| sizes 1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,12,20 x 3 (K 30, n 213) | 0.95 | 0.8 | **0.8620** (4000) | the same (661 `imprecise`) | 0.791 |
| 40 x 20 (n 800) | 0.98 | 0.5 | 0.8982 (4000) | `wilson_refused_clustered` only | 0.868 |

No cell in any of these rows carries `not_evaluable_shown_for_transparency` or `very_low_precision`, because every shape has 30 or more cases. DEC-08 reads: "a cell renders with its tier annotation when the engine's own coverage simulation for that shape is ≥ 0.90, and is refused with a typed reason below it". The brief's Accept line reads: "no rendered clustered cell below the bar without its tier annotation". Both fail on every shape in the table except the last. On the two unequal-size shapes the build made the printed interval worse than the bootstrap it replaced (0.928 to 0.70, 0.923 to 0.855). On realistic unequal sizes `wilson_deff` matched or beat the bootstrap. With 40 or 60 cases of geometric size (1 + Geometric(0.3 / 0.25), largest 20 / 14) it measured 0.9185 / 0.9267 / 0.9283 (p 0.9 / 0.9 / 0.95), against 0.904 / 0.918 / 0.911. With 2 cases of 25 rows and 38 of 2 it measured 0.9100, against 0.899. The equal-size high-correlation shapes were below the bar on both commits. So this is not a claim that the method is worse in general. It is a claim that the page prints it without annotation where its coverage is not known to clear the bar, and on two shapes it is known not to.

The page sentence is false on those shapes. `render/t7.py` `METHOD_DESCRIPTIONS["wilson_deff"]` prints: "printed for a cell of at least five cases, where its measured coverage is at or above the 0.90 bar (section 6)". `test_e11_deff_wilson.py::test_accept_no_rendered_clustered_proportion_below_the_bar_without_its_tier` inspects only that each `wilson_deff` cell has `n_cases >= 5` and route `wilson_deff`. It treats "five cases" as "above the bar" by definition, so it cannot see this. Graded a blocker under the lens rule "a clustered cell below 0.90 rendered without its tier annotation", and because it touches a statistical gate (DEC-12 (i)).

Repro: `PYTHONPATH=<wt>/src python probe/cov_engine.py 4000` (prints `skew40: ... coverage 0.6995; methods {'wilson_deff': 4000}; flags {('wilson_refused_clustered',): 3389, ('imprecise', 'wilson_refused_clustered'): 611}`); `cov_engine3.py 4000` for the TAU2 = 0.8 rows.

What a repair has to decide, not mine to choose: whether the route needs a size-variability or correlation condition, or a second coverage grid (unequal sizes, TAU2 0.8, p 0.95 / 0.98) whose failures keep the tier annotation or refuse under DEC-08. The T7 sentence should name what was measured (equal sizes, TAU2 = 0.5, p 0.5 / 0.9) rather than "where its measured coverage is at or above".
