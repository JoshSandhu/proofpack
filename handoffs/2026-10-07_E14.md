## What was cut and why

- **Item 5 (`80+` age band labels): cut.** Bands are a JSON array of two numbers (`criteria_schema.json` `subgroup_decl.bands`, the same in `output_schema_v1.json`) and `stats.subgroups.band_ages` reads them as `[lo, hi)`. The synthetic `80-200` is the closed band `[80, 200)`; an age of 200 or more is counted in `n_outside_bands`. Printing `80+` would say the band is open, which is false, and a JSON number cannot carry infinity, so an open top band is a schema decision, not a renderer change (build note).
